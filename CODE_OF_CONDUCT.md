# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its code of
conduct. The full text is available at:

- https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
- Source: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/code_of_conduct.md

In short: participants are expected to engage respectfully and in good faith,
and to make participation a welcoming experience for everyone.

## Enforcement

Concerns about conduct may be reported privately to the maintainer at
**manuel.rdrs@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed and handled discreetly.
